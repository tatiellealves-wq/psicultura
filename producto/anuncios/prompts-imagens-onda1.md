# Prompts de imagem — Onda 1 (para ChatGPT / DALL·E)

Formato: **1080×1350 (4:5 vertical)**
Uso: gerar a **foto de fundo**. O texto entra depois no Canva.

---

## ⚠️ Leia antes de gerar

**Não peça o texto em espanhol dentro da imagem.** Os modelos de imagem erram
acentos (`ó`, `í`, `á`, `ñ`) e trocam letras em frases longas. Você recebe
"PRIMFRO EL AGVA" e perde o crédito.

**O jeito certo:** gere a foto limpa com os prompts abaixo — todos já pedem
**espaço vazio proposital** onde o texto vai entrar — e depois coloque o texto
no Canva, com a fonte da marca e os acentos corretos.

**Zona segura no 4:5:** deixe ~150px livres no topo e ~250px na base. A
interface do Instagram cobre essas faixas.

## 🐟 IMAGEM 1 — "¿Primero el tanque o el agua?"

```
Photorealistic vertical photograph, 4:5 aspect ratio, 1080x1350.
A rural backyard in Latin America at early morning, soft golden light.
In the lower third: a partially assembled circular geomembrane fish tank,
still empty, black liner folded over a simple metal frame, a green garden
hose coiled on packed earth beside it. Background: a simple wooden fence,
banana plants and distant hills, softly out of focus.
The upper two thirds is open sky with light morning haze — deliberately
empty negative space reserved for a text overlay.
Natural documentary photography, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field,
muted earthy colours with a teal accent from the liner.
No people. No text. No logos.
Authentic and unpolished — not glossy stock photography.
```

## 💧 IMAGEM 2 — "La semana 2 mata más peces"

```
Photorealistic vertical photograph, 4:5 aspect ratio, 1080x1350.
Extreme close-up of the weathered hands of a Latin American fish farmer
holding a clear plastic bag half-submerged in tank water. Inside the bag,
about a dozen small live tilapia fingerlings — healthy, active, silver-grey
with faint dark stripes. Soft ripples and reflections on the water surface.
Warm late-morning natural light.
Shallow depth of field with focus on the bag; the background is blurred
dark green tank water.
Composition places the hands and bag in the lower half, leaving the upper
half as calm dark water — empty negative space for a text overlay.
Documentary style, warm and honest.
All fish alive and healthy. No dead fish. No faces. No text. No logos.
```

## 💸 IMAGEM 3 — "6 meses gastando"

```
Photorealistic vertical photograph, 4:5 aspect ratio, 1080x1350.
A weathered wooden table outdoors beside a small fish farm. On the table:
an open laptop displaying a spreadsheet with yellow highlighted cells and
columns of figures, a simple pocket calculator, a pencil and a small
notebook. Behind, out of focus, a circular blue-lined tilapia tank in soft
daylight with banana plants around it.
Warm natural light, shallow depth of field, focus on the laptop screen.
Subject sits in the lower two thirds; the top third is open sky and blurred
foliage — empty negative space for a text overlay.
On the screen show ONLY numeric figures, coloured cells and simple bar
charts — absolutely no words, no letters, no readable labels.
Documentary style, authentic small-farm setting. No people. No logos.
```

> ⚠️ A regra "**apenas números, nenhuma palavra**" na tela é essencial. Sem ela
> o modelo inventa texto em espanhol falso e a imagem fica inutilizável.

## 🔁 IMAGEM 4 — "¿Ya lo intentaste?"

```
Photorealistic vertical photograph, 4:5 aspect ratio, 1080x1350.
A Latin American man in his late 40s, weathered face, simple worn work
shirt and a cap, standing at the edge of a small tilapia tank, looking down
at the water with a thoughtful, reflective expression — not sad, not
smiling, simply considering.
Late afternoon golden light coming from behind him, long soft shadows.
He occupies the right side of the frame, in the lower two thirds. The left
side and the top of the image are open — calm water surface and warm sky —
deliberately empty negative space for a text overlay.
Documentary photojournalism style, shot on 50mm, natural skin texture, no
retouching or beauty smoothing. Authentic rural setting.
No text. No logos.
```

## Se quiser mesmo o texto embutido

Só para frases **curtas, sem acento**. Acrescente ao final do prompt:

```
Include the following text rendered in a bold heavy sans-serif font,
in white with a subtle dark shadow, centered in the upper empty area,
spelled EXACTLY as written here: "TEXTO AQUI"
Render this text perfectly and legibly. No other text anywhere.
```

Gere 3 ou 4 variações e escolha a que soletrou certo. Ainda assim, o Canva
continua sendo mais confiável.

---

## Montagem no Canva

1. Sobe a foto em 1080×1350
2. Gradiente escuro por cima (preto, 0→60% de opacidade, de baixo para cima)
   para o texto branco ganhar contraste
3. Texto no terço central, fonte grossa, tamanho grande
4. Selo do preço na base, acima dos 250px de zona segura
5. Textos exatos de cada peça: `criativos-estaticos-onda1.md`
